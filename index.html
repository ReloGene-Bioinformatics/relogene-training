What this portal contains

This training portal is intended to provide organized access to company training material across different bioinformatics modalities. Each module page can be expanded to include:

  • Downloadable PowerPoint slide decks
  • R, Python, or shell scripts
  • Standard workflows and SOP-style notes
  • Example datasets and practice material
  • Recommended software and setup instructions
